# Privacy Policy - SEO Analyzer Tools

**Last Updated:** December 18, 2025

## Overview
SEO Analyzer Tools is a browser extension that analyzes web pages for search engine optimization (SEO) elements. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This extension operates with a privacy-first approach: all analysis happens locally in your browser, and we do not collect, store, or transmit your browsing data.

## Data Collection
SEO Analyzer Tools does NOT collect, store, or transmit any of your browsing data or personal information. All SEO analysis happens locally in your browser.

## What We Analyze (Locally Only)
When you activate the extension on a web page, it reads the following content from the current page for analysis:
- Meta tags (title, description, Open Graph, Twitter/X cards)
- Structured data (JSON-LD schemas)
- Heading structure (H1-H6 tags)
- Images and alt text

**Important:** This data is processed entirely in your browser and is never sent to our servers or any third party.

## What We Do NOT Collect
- Pages you visit or analyze
- SEO data from websites
- Search history or browsing history
- Personal information (name, email, address, etc.)
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Keystroke or mouse tracking
- IP addresses or location data
- Financial information or payment details

## Payment Processing
Premium subscriptions are processed through ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com), a third-party payment processor. When you purchase a premium subscription, ExtensionPay handles all payment information. We do not have access to your payment details.

Please refer to ExtensionPay's privacy policy at https://extensionpay.com/privacy for payment-related data handling.

## Browser Permissions
This extension requests the following permissions:

- **scripting** - Required to analyze page content for SEO data
- **activeTab** - Required to access the current webpage you're viewing
- **storage** - Required to remember your premium subscription status locally

## Local Storage Only
Your premium subscription status is stored locally in your browser only. This data never leaves your device.

## Chrome Web Store Compliance
This extension complies with Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies:

### Data Usage Certification

- ✅ We do NOT sell or transfer user data to third parties (except ExtensionPay for payment processing)

### Single Purpose
This extension has a single, narrow purpose: to analyze web pages for SEO elements and display the results to help users optimize their content for search engines.
